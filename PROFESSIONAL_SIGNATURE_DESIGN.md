## 📸 **Professional Image Setup**

### **Authorized Signature:**
- **What:** Scanned image of authorized person's signature
- **Size:** 80-120px height recommended
- **Resolution:** 300 DPI (print quality)
- **Format:** PNG with transparent background
- **Appearance:** Professional, clean signature

**How to Prepare:**
1. Obtain original signature on white paper
2. Scan at 300 DPI (or use professional scanner)
3. Use Photoshop/GIMP to remove white background (make transparent)
4. Export as PNG
5. Crop to remove excess space
6. Final size: ~100-150px width × 80-100px height

### **Company Seal:**
- **What:** Official company seal, emblem, or stamp
- **Size:** 150x150px to 250x250px (square or circular)
- **Resolution:** 300 DPI (print quality)
- **Format:** PNG with transparent background
- **Appearance:** Professional seal/emblem with company name

**How to Prepare:**
1. Obtain high-resolution seal image (from your designer/branding)
2. If physical seal: scan at 300 DPI
3. Use Photoshop/GIMP to remove background (make transparent)
4. Ensure it's circular or square (symmetrical)
5. Export as PNG
6. Final size: ~200x200px recommended

## 🚀 **Setup Steps**

### **Step 1: Prepare Images**
- Scan authorized signature at 300 DPI
- Scan company seal at 300 DPI
- Remove white backgrounds (make transparent) using:
  - Photoshop
  - GIMP (free)
  - Online tool: remove.bg

### **Step 2: Upload to Settings**
1. Go to **Admin → Settings → PDF & Document Settings**
2. Find **"Professional Signature & Company Seal"** section
3. Upload **Authorized Signature** image
4. Upload **Company Seal** image
5. Review the professional guidelines shown
6. Click **"Save Changes"**

### **Step 3: Test in Quotation PDF**
1. Create a test quotation
2. Click **"Print / Save PDF"**
3. Verify signature and seal appear professionally
4. Check alignment and sizing
5. Make adjustments if needed

## 💡 **Professional Tips**

### **For Signature:**
✅ **DO:**
- Use actual authorized person's signature
- Scan at high resolution (300 DPI)
- Keep background clean and transparent
- Position signature prominently

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use typed signature
- Use low-resolution scan
- Include unnecessary background
- Make signature too small

### **For Company Seal:**
✅ **DO:**
- Use official company seal/emblem
- Ensure it's centered and clean
- Use high-quality scan (300 DPI)
- Make it circular or symmetrical

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use temporary stickers
- Use poor quality copies
- Make seal look pixelated
- Use overly complex designs

## 🔧 **Troubleshooting**

### **Signature/Seal not showing?**
1. Verify image was uploaded successfully
2. Check image format is PNG (not JPG)
3. Clear browser cache
4. Try re-uploading image

### **Image looks blurry?**
1. Ensure scanned at 300 DPI
2. Try with higher resolution image
3. Reduce image size slightly
4. Use clear, sharp scans

### **Background not transparent?**
1. Use GIMP or Photoshop to remove background
2. Export as PNG with transparency
3. Test with remove.bg online tool
4. Verify transparency in image viewer

### **Layout looks wrong?**
1. Check image dimensions are appropriate
2. Ensure images aren't too large
3. Use recommended sizes (sig: 100px height, seal: 200px)
4. Test in different PDF viewers

## 📱 **Print Tips**

For best results:
1. Use Chrome to print/save PDF
2. Enable "Print backgrounds" in print dialog
3. Use "Save as PDF" rather than physical printer
4. Set margins to normal or minimal
5. Don't scale or fit-to-page
